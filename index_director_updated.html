About Us

Star Light International School is committed to providing quality education in a caring, disciplined and inspiring environment for children from Nursery to Class 10. Under the direction of Er. Md Abdul Quadir Rizvi, the school aims to build strong academic foundations, encourage curiosity and creativity, develop confidence and nurture responsible, value-driven learners. We believe every child has unique potential and deserves the opportunity to shine.
